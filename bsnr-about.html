Our Story

The storybehind BSNR

When the EU Deforestation Regulation came into force, it placed a new infrastructure and compliance cost on the entire rubber value chain, a cost that smallholder farmers were least equipped to carry on their own. Rather than let that burden fall on individual farmers and small businesses, the Rubber Board of India, along with TRST01, launched Bharat Sustainable Natural Rubber (BSNR) to safeguard the industry with one coordinated national platform.

The partnership was deliberate in its design: a single system and standard for the whole industry instead of fragmented, company-led compliance, data held centrally under the Rubber Board with stakeholder identities protected through unique BSNR IDs, and a platform built for first-time digital users rather than large enterprises alone.

When I first heard about the new EUDR regulation, I felt a deep fear. I have always farmed legally in a non-deforested area, yet the thought of losing access to markets because of documents and technology I had never seen before was overwhelming. The BSNR initiative has come as a relief, giving us the confidence that even a smallholder farmer like me can meet the global requirements.

Padmanabhan · natural rubber smallholder farmer, Ernakulam district, Kerala
National Recognition

India's Official Answer to EUDR

BSNR's momentum earned national endorsement when the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India, designated the Rubber Board as the nodal agency for EUDR compliance for rubber, making BSNR the country's official, nation-level answer to the regulation.

Technology Partner

Built in Partnership with TRST01

BSNR is developed and operated in partnership between the Rubber Board of India and TRST01, which serves as the platform's technology and implementation partner, from field data collection and satellite verification to the digital infrastructure that generates each Due Diligence Statement.

Founded in 2019 and headquartered in Hyderabad, India, with a global presence spanning Singapore, Australia and the United States, TRST01 is a sustainable technology company specialising in supply chain transparency, ESG management and regulatory compliance. Its guiding principle, "Trust through Technology", drives a mission to help businesses and governments adopt sustainable, ethical and climate-conscious practices across their supply chains.

The company's work has been recognised with honours including the Sustainability Impact Award 2024, a place in the Forbes India D-Globalist Select200, and the Aegis Graham Bell Award 2025. TRST01's EUDR products span the supply chain: TRST01Chain for plot mapping and Due Diligence Statement generation at the exporter and processor level, TRST01Chain MeeS for vendor compliance and internal traceability at the manufacturer level, and the TRST01Chain EUDR Hub for supplier onboarding and Due Diligence Statement filing on the EU importer side.

FAQ

Frequently askedquestions

What is BSNR?

BSNR, Bharat Sustainable Natural Rubber, is India's national digital platform for EUDR compliance and sustainability certification in the natural rubber sector, connecting farmers, dealers, processors, manufacturers and exporters on one shared system.

Who runs the BSNR platform?

BSNR is a partnership between the Rubber Board of India and TRST01, which serves as the programme's technology and implementation partner, covering field data collection, satellite verification and digital compliance filing.

Is there a cost for farmers to join BSNR?

No. Registration, plot mapping, risk assessment and documentation are free for every farmer on the platform.

How does BSNR verify that a plot is deforestation free?

Every plot record passes through independent field validation and satellite-based deforestation analysis against the EUDR's 31 December 2020 baseline before it is used in any shipment's compliance documentation.

How does data get collected from smallholder farmers?

Trained field agents visit farmers directly to complete registration, consent and plot boundary mapping. The process works fully offline in areas without connectivity and does not require the farmer to install anything.

Which regions does BSNR cover?

BSNR is being rolled out across India's rubber-growing states, with mapping and onboarding conducted in phases starting with the major rubber-producing regions of Kerala before expanding nationally.

What is TRST01?

TRST01 is a global EUDR compliance software and services provider, used by more than 200 businesses and governments across more than 10 countries to manage supply chain traceability, deforestation risk assessment and regulatory reporting.

What products does TRST01 offer for EUDR compliance?

TRST01 offers TRST01Chain for exporters and processors, TRST01Chain MeeS for manufacturers, and the TRST01Chain EUDR Hub for EU importers, covering plot mapping, vendor compliance and Due Diligence Statement filing across the supply chain.
