About EUDR

EUDR is a regulation implemented by the European Union to address the issue of deforestation and forest degradation caused by agricultural commodity production. It is a significant step forward in the fight against deforestation, and is expected to have a major impact on the global supply chains of agricultural commodities.

Critical aspects ofEUDR compliance

01

Deforestation Analysis

To identify and assess the risk of deforestation and forest degradation associated with an exporter's supply chain.

Learn more
02

Risk & Legality Assessments

To evaluate the legal and regulatory risks associated with the exporter's supply chain, including compliance with local laws and regulations.

Learn more
03

Traceability & Due Diligence

To ensure that products can be traced back to their source of origin and that complete segregation has been maintained till the export of rubber products.
